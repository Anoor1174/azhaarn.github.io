Invisible Strings: How AI Bias Hides in Proxy Variables

Testing whether proxy relationships planted at varying strengths in synthetic datasets can be reliably detected.

Abstract

Bias is learned through socialisation, which AI systems can later inherit. Removing a protected attribute such as ethnicity or gender from a dataset does not eliminate underlying bias, because seemingly neutral features—such as postcodes or job titles—can act as proxy variables that encode it. This project asks whether such proxies can be detected reliably by generating synthetic datasets with planted proxy relationships of varying strength.
